BSU Prints: Printing and Design in Karachi

#1 Creative & Professional Printing & Designing Service Provider in Karachi.

BSU Prints is Pakistan’s 1st online eCommerce printing store and online business card maker, based at Shop #5, Alvi Mansion, Outram Road, Pakistan Chowk, Karachi. We were previously known as StrokeArt and PrintShopKarachi. Today we design and print business cards, stationery, brochures, catalogues, flyers, envelopes, stickers, labels, packaging, calendars, booklets and panaflex signage for businesses across Pakistan.

Business

Our aim is simple: premium printing at fair rates, with design help whenever you need it. Whether you want a hundred visiting cards or a full set of company stationery, we handle design, printing and delivery, with free express shipping in Karachi. Starting a new business? Ask about our Designer at Your Door Step service for your logo, business cards, letterhead, envelope and brochure. Explore business cards, custom labels, packaging or stationery, or request a quote.

History

BSU Prints started on August 14, 1975 as StrokeArt, serving customers with business cards, visiting cards, stationery and brochures. On March 23, 2000 it was renamed PrintShopKarachi and expanded into catalogues, company profiles and more. On February 14, 2016 it became BSUPrints, moving online and extending its services to a national level. Today BSUPrints offers complete online design and printing solutions and ships its products across Pakistan and worldwide.
