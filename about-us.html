BSU Prints

#1 Creative & Professional Printing & Designing Service Provider in Karachi.

History

BSU Prints was started August 14, 1975 as StorkeArt. That was StrokeArt was very famous by their amazing services, StorkeArt was serving their customers by Business Cards, Visiting Cards, Stationery, Brochures and much more services. In March 23, 2000, it was renamed as PrintShopKarachi and started service again and this time they increased their services by providing Cataloque, Company Profiles printing and other services. Finally in February 14, 2016 it was again renamed as BSUPrints, and this time as BSU Prints they even expand their services to national level.
