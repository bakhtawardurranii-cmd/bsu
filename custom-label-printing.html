Custom Labels questions answered

Which label material should I choose?
For bottles and anything that meets water or oil, choose vinyl or waterproof labels. For dry goods, matte or gloss paper labels work well. Tell us the product and how it is stored and we will recommend a material.
Do you print roll labels and sheet labels?
Yes. We print roll labels, sheet labels and die-cut labels in custom shapes and sizes.
